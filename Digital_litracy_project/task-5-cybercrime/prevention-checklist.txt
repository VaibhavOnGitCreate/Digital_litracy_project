Stay Safe Online — Prevention Checklist

1. Never share OTP or UPI PIN
2. Do not click suspicious links
3. Verify unknown payment requests
4. Use official banking apps only
5. Enable two-factor authentication
6. Do not share personal information online
7. Check website URLs carefully
8. Report suspicious activity immediately
9. Use secure internet connections
10. Update apps regularly
