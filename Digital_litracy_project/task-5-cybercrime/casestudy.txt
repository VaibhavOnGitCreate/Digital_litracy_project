Cybercrime Case Study: UPI Payment Fraud

UPI payment fraud is one of the most common cybercrimes in India. In this type of fraud, 
scammers trick users into sharing sensitive details such as OTPs, UPI PINs, or clicking malicious payment links.
A common method involves fraudsters posing as bank officials or customer support representatives.

For example, a student receives a call claiming that their bank account needs verification. 
The caller asks the student to click a link or share an OTP. Once the student shares the information, 
the fraudster gains access to the bank account and transfers money instantly. 
Since UPI transactions are immediate, recovering the money becomes very difficult.

Students and young users are often targeted because they frequently use digital payment apps. 
The consequences include financial loss, stress, and compromised personal information. 
This type of cybercrime highlights the importance of awareness and careful online behavior. 
Understanding such fraud helps students recognize suspicious activities and avoid becoming victims.
